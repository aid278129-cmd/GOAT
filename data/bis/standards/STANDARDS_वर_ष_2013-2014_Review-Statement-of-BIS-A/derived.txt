REVIEW OF BIS ANNUAL REPORT 2013-14 
 
 
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) was set up on 01 April 1987 as a statutory body, taking over 
the functions of the erstwhile Indian Standards Institution (ISI). Its major activities include Formulation 
of Standards, Product Certification, Hallmarking, various Management Systems Certification 
Schemes, Laboratory Services, Sale of Indian Standards and Training services.  Besides, it is also 
engaged in Consumer related and Promotional Activities.  
 
Major achievements of BIS during the year 2013-14, as reported in its Annual Report for this period 
are summarized below. 
 
 STANDARDIZATION                                           
 
BIS is formulating need-based Indian standards in line with the National Priorities in a time-bound 
manner. It also harmonizes National Standards with International Standards in order to facilitate 
adoption of International Standards by all segments of industry and business.  
 
During 2013-14, BIS formulated 547 standards bringing the total number of standards in force to 
19199 as on 31 March 2014.  In addition, 4436 standards were reviewed.  5259 Indian standards 
have been harmonized with International Standards so far (i.e. about 88.5% where corresponding 
ISO/IEC standards exist). During the year, BIS also organized 13 Seminars/ Workshops and 
participated in Conferences with a view to disseminate information about the availability of standards 
and to get feedback for further improvement/ updating as well as to find out fields where 
standardization is needed by the industry. 
 
CERTIFICATION 
 
 PRODUCT CERTIFICATION   
 
During the year 2013-14, BIS has granted 3580 new licences (excluding hallmarking). The total 
number of operative licences as on 31 March 2014 was 27887. Ten new products were covered for the 
first time under product certification. Under foreign manufacturers’ certification scheme, 147 licences 
were granted, thus making a total of 379 licences located in various countries. 
 
Registration Scheme  
 
BIS has recently introduced Registration Scheme for 15 Electronics and Information Technology 
Goods (like LED/LCD TVs, Laptops, Printers, Set Top Box, Microwave Oven, Notebook/Tablets, 
Projectors, Servers, Storage, etc), notified by the Department of Electronics and Information 
Technology, Govt. of India, under the Compulsory Registration Scheme for Self declaration of 
conformity. Registration for self-declaration by the manufacturer is a simplified process of conformity 
assessment, which has been introduced, and is in addition to the existing system of licencing. BIS 
has granted 750 registrations to manufacturers located throughout the world, since its launch upto 31 
Mar 2014. 
 
Review Meetings  
 
In order to acquire feedback on the operation of the BIS Certification Marks Scheme, review meetings 
with the licensees are organized on a regular basis. In 2013-14, 20 review meetings were organized.

HALLMARKING OF JEWELLERY/ ARTEFACTS 
 
The Scheme for Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery, which was successfully launched by BIS in April 
2000, continued to make considerable progress. 1666 Gold hallmarking licences and 193 Silver 
hallmarking licences have been granted during the year. The number of operative licences for 
Hallmarking of gold and silver jewellery was 11902 and 841 respectively, as on 31 March 2014.  The 
number of BIS recognized assaying and hallmarking centres was 285 as on 31 March 2014. Regional 
and Branch offices of BIS organized 33 Awareness programmes for jewellers.  
 
 
 
MANAGEMENT  SYSTEMS CERTIFICATION   
 
BIS continued to provide the following Management Systems Certification services as per the 
corresponding standards. 49 Quality Management System Certification licences, 14 Environmental 
Management Systems Certification licences, 08 Occupational Health & Safety Management Systems 
Certification licences, 01 Food Safety Management System Certification licence and 14 Service 
Quality Management System Certification licences were granted during the year. As on 31 March 
2014, the total number of operative licences was 1247. 
 
LABORATORY SERVICES 
 
The network of eight BIS laboratories located in different regions of the country continued to 
undertake conformity testing of BIS certified products against relevant Indian standards. The number 
of samples tested and the number of test reports issued by BIS Laboratories were 15990 and 18622 
respectively. Gold Referral Assaying Laboratory at Chennai started since December 2010, has issued 
609 test reports during 2013 - 14. 
 
BIS Lab Recognition Scheme 
 
BIS lab recognition scheme supports operation of product certification scheme of BIS.  The services 
of outside laboratories are availed in an eventuality of heavy load for testing in BIS Labs, any 
equipment being out of order or test facility not being there. As on 31 March 2013 there were 147 
outside laboratories recognized by BIS. Besides, facilities of 24 Government laboratories (specialized 
labs/ other labs) are being utilized by BIS as and when required. To support newly launched BIS 
Registration Scheme, 11 outside labs were recognized for testing of IT products 
 
i-Care 
 
To deal with consumer the related activities in a more focused manner and for creating greater 
impact, departments of BIS having direct interface with the public and consumers namely, Consumer 
Affairs Department, Enforcement Department, Public relations Department and Sales department at 
BIS Head Quarters have been placed under a single activity. Besides, the activities of Standards 
Promotion, Consumer Affairs, Enforcement, Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Award and other such 
awards have been placed in the newly created i-Care Department by merging the existing Consumer 
Affairs and Enforcement Department and transferring work related to Rajiv Gandhi National Quality 
Award and other Awards. 
 
STANDARDS PROMOTION AND CONSUMER AFFAIRS ACTIVITIES 
 
During the year, 115 Consumer Awareness Programmes, 28 Industry Awareness Programmes and 
25 Educational Utilization of Standards Programmes were organised.

Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Award  
 
With a view to encourage manufacturers and service organizations to strive for excellence, Rajiv 
Gandhi National Quality Award was instituted by the Bureau in 1991. The fact finding and evaluation 
process of short listed applicant organizations for RGNQA 2012 have been completed by the 
Regional Evaluation Committees. The meeting of the National Awards Committee (NAC) was 
organized under the chairmanship of Secretary (CA) to decide the award winners and recipients of 
commendation certificates.  Awards will be presented at the award function, to be organized. 
 
World Standards Day  
 
World Standards Day was celebrated on 14 October 2013 to commemorate the establishment of the 
International Organization for Standardization (ISO). The theme of this year World Standards Day 
was “International Standards Ensure Positive Change”. 
 
ENFORCEMENT ACTIVITIES 
 
In order to protect the common consumer from being cheated by those misusing BIS Standard Mark 
97 raids were carried out all over the country. 66 cases filed in court were decided, out of which 63 
cases were decided in favour of BIS. 
 
INTERNATIONAL ACTIVITIES 
 
BIS, in its capacity as the National Standards Body of India, is a member of International Organization 
for Standardization (ISO) and International Electro-technical Commission (IEC). It is actively involved 
in the development of International Standards by acting as Participating (P) member or Observer (O) 
member on various Technical Committees, Sub-Committees, Working Groups, etc 
 
INFORMATION SERVICES 
 
Technical Information Service Centre 
 
Technical Information Services are provided to Industry, importers, exporters, individuals and 
government agencies in response to their enquiries. In this endeavour, more than 400 enquiries were 
responded during the year 2013-14. 
 
WTO/TBT Enquiry Point 
 
BIS continues to handle the responsibilities of WTO-TBT Enquiry Point, as designated by Ministry of 
Commerce.  During the period TBT Notifications were disseminated and efforts were made to 
sensitize the stakeholders 
 
TRAINING SERVICES 
During the period 01 April 2013 – 31 March 2014, National Institute of Training for Standardization 
(NITS) organized 47 In-house programmes, 29 Open programmes including 10 Lead Auditors 
courses for the industry thus generating revenue of around Rs. 1.59 crores. Besides above, 03 
International Training Programmes and 16 programmes for BIS employees were also organised.

VIGILANCE ACTIVITIES 
 
Vigilance set up of BIS is headed by the Chief Vigilance Officer (CVO) and comprises of Vigilance 
Department at the Headquarters and seven Vigilance Sections in the secretariat of each of the 
Disciplinary Authority for Ground B, C & D employees (Deputy Director General concerned). Training 
programmes on “Preventive Vigilance” was designed covering various aspects related to vigilance 
matters for senior officers of BIS, which were conducted at 05 different places, all over the Country. 
Vigilance Awareness Week was celebrated in BIS from 28 October to 02 November 2013.

HUMAN RESOURCES 
 
76 scientists have been recruited for the post of Scientist-B by direct recruitment, whereas 01 post of

INCOME AND EXPENDITURE  
 
For the Twenty Fifth consecutive year i.e. 2013-14, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) continued to be 
self reliant in meeting its expenditure and other liabilities.  Total income (excluding income from 
investment) during the year 2013-14 was Rs. 31443.12 lakh against Rs.28017.48 lakh in the previous 
year resulting in an increase of 12.23%, which includes income from the BIS Registration Scheme. 
The largest contribution to the income was from ISI Certification Marking Fee which stood at Rs. 
27949.15 lakh against Rs. 24695.09 lakh in the previous year i.e. an increase of 13.18%. The total 
revenue expenditure during the year 2013-14 stood at Rs.18656.30 lakh as against Rs. 17838.12 
lakh in 2012-13 registering an increase of 4.59%.
