REVIEW OF BIS ANNUAL REPORT 2011-12 
 
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) was set up on 1 April 1987 as a statutory body, taking 
over the functions of the erstwhile Indian Standards Institution (ISI).  Its major activities 
include Formulation of Standards, Product Certification, Hallmarking, various Management 
Systems Certification Schemes, Laboratory Services, Sale of Indian Standards and Training 
services.  Besides, it is also engaged in Consumer related and Promotional Activities.  
 
Major achievements of BIS during the year 2011-12, as reported in its Annual Report for this 
period are summarized below. 
 
  FORMULATION AND REVIEW OF STANDARDS                                           
 
During 2011-12, BIS formulated 410 standards bringing the total number of standards in 
force to 18742 as on 31 March 2012.  In addition, 3784 standards were reviewed.  5091 
Indian standards have been harmonized with International Standards so far (i.e. about 88% 
where corresponding ISO/IEC standards exist). In all 26 Seminars/ Conferences/ Workshops 
were conducted to disseminate information on standards available in the specific fields for 
increased implementation. 
 
  PRODUCT CERTIFICATION   
 
During the year 2011-12, BIS has granted 3099 new licences (excluding hallmarking). The 
total number of operative licences as on 31 March 2012 was 25330. During the year, 11 new 
products were covered for the first time under product certification. 
 
Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme  
 
BIS is operating two schemes for certification of imported goods, one for foreign 
manufacturers and the other for Indian importers.  During the year, 73 licences were granted 
to foreign manufacturers making the total of 232 licences located in various countries. 
 
Review Meetings  
 
In order to acquire feedback on the operation of the BIS Certification Marks Scheme, review 
meetings with the licensees are organized on a regular basis. In 2011-12, 10 review 
meetings were organized with licensees of Packaged Drinking Water; Steel and Steel 
Products; Cement and Allied Products; Tyres; Electrical Cables and Conductors; LPG 
Cylinders, Jute and Jute Products; Plastic Pipes and Plastic Related Products; Fire 
Extinguishers; etc.

April 2000, continued to make considerable progress. Its simplification and rationalization has 
resulted in an increase to 9292 jewellers as on 31 March 2012 from 8098 jewellers as on 31 
March 2011. The number of silver licences for Hallmarking of silver jewellery/ artefacts has

MANAGEMENT  SYSTEMS CERTIFICATION   
 
Quality Management System Certification 
 
During 2011-12, 119 Quality Management System Certification(QMSC) licences have been 
granted, covering  industrial sectors like chemicals, metal and metal products, cement, 
construction, dairy plants, electricity generation, engineering services, mining, machinery, 
petroleum, plastic, pharmaceuticals, textiles, and service sectors such as education, financial 
sector, health sector, insurance, information technology, telecommunications, transport  etc.  
The total number of operative licences as on 31 March 2012 was 937.

technology areas like Integrated Steel Plants, Thermal Power Plants, Aeronautical Industries,

Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems (OHSMS) Certification  
 
BIS launched OHSMS certification as per IS 18001:2000 in January 2003. It essentially 
enables an organization to define, plan and manage its objectives and policies, taking into 
account legislative requirements and information about significant hazards and risks, so as to 
protect its employees and others, whose health and safety may be affected by the activities 
of the organization. During the year, 9 OHSMS licences have been granted making the total 
licences in operation as 56.  The licences cover areas like Thermal Power Plants, Ceramic 
Industry, Cycle Industry, Gas Power Station, Health Services and Employee Development 
Centre.

ENFORCEMENT ACTIVITIES 
 
In order to protect the common consumer from being cheated by those misusing BIS 
Standard Mark 125 raids were carried out all over the country. During these raids various 
spurious products including many household products such as Packaged Drinking Water, 
Block Board and Plywood products, PVC Cables, Switches & Sockets, Electrical Appliance, 
Submersible pumps, Cement, Cattle feeds, Iodized salt, Steel Tubes, Cast Iron Sluice 
Valves, LPG Regulators, HDPE Pipes etc. were seized.   
 
LABORATORY SERVICES 
 
The network of eight BIS laboratories located in different regions of the country continued to 
undertake conformity testing of BIS certified products against relevant Indian standards.  
During the year, 19005 test reports were issued by BIS laboratories covering wide range of 
products. Gold Referral Assaying Laboratory at Chennai has issued 226 test reports from 1 
April 2011 to 31 March 2012. It has also participated in Inter Laboratory Comparison 
programme for testing of samples of Gold jewellery with satisfactory results.  
 
BIS Lab Recognition Scheme 
 
BIS lab recognition scheme supports operation of product certification scheme of BIS.  The 
services of outside laboratories are availed in an eventuality of heavy load for testing in BIS 
Labs, any equipment being out of order or test facility not being there. As on as on 31 March 
2012 there were 131 outside laboratories recognized by BIS. Besides, facilities of 24 
Government laboratories (specialized labs/ other labs) are being utilized by BIS as and when 
required.  
 
Based on the lab recognition experience, Lab Recognition Scheme (LRS) document has 
been revised and will replace existing LRS document after approval from the Executive 
Committee. 
 
Modernization and Up gradation of test facilities 
 
Testing facilities for Flat Plate Solar Collector has been set up at Bengaluru lab with the 
financial grant from Ministry of Non-renewable Energy Sources (MNES). 
 
In the modernization programme, the Standing Committee had approved for modernization, 
completion of partial test facilities, up gradation of test facilities, development of new test 
facilities and infrastructure for BIS laboratories.  Under this programme equipments to the 
tune of around ` 4.80 crores have been purchased.

INFORMATION SERVICES 
 
Technical Information Service Centre 
 
Technical Information Services are provided to Industry, importers, exporters, individuals and 
government agencies in response to their enquiries. In this endeavour, more than 350 
enquiries were responded during the year 2011-12. 
 
WTO/TBT Enquiry Point 
 
BIS continues to handle the responsibilities of WTO-TBT Enquiry Point, as designated by 
Ministry of Commerce.  During the period TBT Notifications were disseminated and efforts 
were made to   sensitize the stakeholders 
 
OTHER ACTIVITIES 
       
(a) 
With a view to propagating the concept of standardization and quality systems 
amongst small scale industries, industry awareness programmes are organized by 
BIS.  During the year, 14 such programmes were organized. BIS is creating 
awareness among consumers about its activities by conducting consumer 
awareness programmes through its network of regional and branch offices and by 
participation in seminars/conferences organized by consumer organizations. 
During the year, 135 such programmes were organized. In addition, 4 programmes 
for Educational Utilization of Standards were also organized to propagate the 
message of standardization and create awareness about latest Indian Standards in 
various professional institutes and universities throughout the country. 
 
World Standards Day 2011 was celebrated on 14th October 2011 on the theme 
“International Standards- Creating Confidence Globally” to commemorate the 
establishment of International Organization for Standardization (ISO).  To mark this 
occasion, seminars, exhibitions, lectures, meetings and open house programmes 
were organized at Headquarters and Regional/Branch/Inspection Offices of BIS. 
 
(b) 
Hindi Pakhwara was celebrated during 14-28 September 2011 wherein various 
competitions in Hindi were organized.   
 
(c) 
To spread awareness of BIS activities, programmes covering various BIS functions 
were telecast. In addition programmes on BIS activities were also broadcast from 
various kendras of AIR.     
 
(d)

Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi.  368 delegates from 127 countries and international 
organizations participated in the General Assembly. During the General Assembly 
week, 45th Plenary of ISO DEVCO was also organized.

INCOME AND EXPENDITURE  
 
For the Twenty Third consecutive year i.e. 2011-12, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) 
continued to be self reliant in meeting its expenditure and other liabilities.  Total income 
(excluding income from investment) during the year 2011-12 was Rs.25266.26 lakh against 
Rs.22624.16 lakhs in the previous year resulting in an increase of 11.68%.  The largest 
contribution to the income was from ISI Certification Marking Fee which stood at 
Rs.22393.16  lakh  against Rs. 20012.25 lakh in the previous year i.e. an increase of 
11.90%. The total  revenue expenditure during the year 2011-12 stood at Rs.16970.32 lakh 
as against Rs. 15401.71 lakh during 2010-11 registering an increase of 10.18%.
