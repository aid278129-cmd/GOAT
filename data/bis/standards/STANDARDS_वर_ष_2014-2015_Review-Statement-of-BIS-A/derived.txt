REVIEW OF BIS ANNUAL REPORT 2014-15 
 
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) was set up on 01 April 1987 as a statutory body, 
taking over the functions of the erstwhile Indian Standards Institution (ISI). Its major activities 
include Formulation of Standards, Product Certification, Hallmarking, various Management 
Systems Certification Schemes, Laboratory Services, Sale of Indian Standards and Training 
services.  Besides, it is also engaged in Consumer related and Promotional Activities.  
 
Major achievements of BIS during the year 2014-15, as reported in its Annual Report are 
summarized below: 
 
 STANDARDIZATION                                           
 
BIS is formulating need-based Indian standards in line with the National Priorities in a time-
bound manner. It also harmonizes National Standards with International Standards in order 
to facilitate adoption of International Standards by all segments of industry and business.  
 
During 2014-15, BIS formulated 746 standards bringing the total number of standards in 
force to 19295 as on 31 March 2015.  In addition, 3228 standards were reviewed.  5249 
Indian standards have been harmonized with International Standards so far. During the year, 
BIS also organized 12 Seminars/ Workshops and participated in Conferences with a view to 
disseminate information about the availability of standards and to get feedback for further 
improvement/ updating as well as to find out fields where standardization is needed by the 
industry. 
 
 
CERTIFICATION 
 
 PRODUCT CERTIFICATION   
 
During the year 2014-15, BIS has granted 3202 new licences (excluding hallmarking). The 
total number of operative licences as on 31 March 2014 was 29516. Ten new products were 
covered for the first time under product certification. Under foreign manufacturers’ 
certification scheme, 111 licences were granted, thus making a total of 470 licences located 
in various countries. 
 
Registration Scheme  
 
Registration Scheme is being operated by BIS as per BIS Rules - Chapter IV A 
“REGISTRATION”. The Department of Electronics and Information Technology, Ministry of 
Communication & Information Technology, had issued “Electronics and Information 
Technology Goods (Requirements for Compulsory Registration) Order, 2012” notifying 15 
products for compulsory registration w.e.f. 03 April 2013. Govt. of India, has notified 15 more 
Electronics and IT goods during Nov 2014, taking the total number of products under this 
scheme to 30. As on 31 Mar 2015, a total of 1428 registrations have been granted to 
manufacturers located throughout the world for the above mentioned products.

Review Meetings  
In order to acquire feedback on the operation of the BIS Certification Marks Scheme, review 
meetings with the licensees are organized on a regular basis. In 2014-15, 60 review 
meetings were organized. 
 
LABORATORY SERVICES 
 
The network of eight BIS laboratories located in different regions of the country continued to 
undertake conformity testing of BIS certified products against relevant Indian standards. The 
number of samples tested by BIS Laboratories was 14966. Gold Referral Assaying 
Laboratory at Chennai started since December 2010, has issued 1174 test reports during 
2014-15. As on March 2015, 143 outside labs stood recognised by BIS. 
 
BIS Lab Recognition Scheme 
 
BIS lab recognition scheme supports operation of product certification scheme of BIS.  The 
services of outside laboratories are availed in an eventuality of heavy load for testing in BIS 
Labs, any equipment being out of order or test facility not being there. As on 31 March 2015, 
there were 143 outside laboratories recognized by BIS. Besides, facilities of 46 Government 
laboratories (specialized labs/ other labs) are being utilized by BIS as and when required. To 
support newly launched BIS Registration Scheme, 93 outside labs were recognized for 
testing of IT products. 
 
 
HALLMARKING OF JEWELLERY/ ARTEFACTS 
 
The Scheme for Hallmarking of Gold Jewellery, which was successfully launched by BIS in 
April 2000, continued to make considerable progress. 1995 Gold hallmarking licences and 
176 Silver hallmarking licences have been granted during the year. The number of operative 
licences for Hallmarking of gold and silver jewellery was 13112 and 936 respectively, as on 
31 March 2015.  The number of BIS recognized assaying and hallmarking centres was 331 
as on 31 March 2015. Regional and Branch offices of BIS organized 40 Awareness 
programmes for jewellers.  
 
 
 
MANAGEMENT  SYSTEMS CERTIFICATION   
 
BIS continued to provide the following Management Systems Certification services as per the 
corresponding standards. 53 Quality Management System Certification licences, 27 
Environmental Management Systems Certification licences, 13 Occupational Health & Safety 
Management Systems Certification licences, 01 Food Safety Management System 
Certification licence, 17 Service Quality Management System Certification licences and 06 
Energy Management System Certification licences were granted during the year. As on 31 
March 2015, the total number of operative licences was 1295. 
 
INTERNATIONAL ACTIVITIES 
 
BIS, in its capacity as the National Standards Body of India, is a member of International 
Organization for Standardization (ISO) and International Electro-technical Commission (IEC). 
It is actively involved in the development of International Standards by acting as Participating 
(P) member or Observer (O) member on various Technical Committees, Sub-Committees, 
Working Groups, etc.

Information services 
 
Technical Information Service Centre 
 
Technical Information Services are provided to Industry, importers, exporters, individuals and 
government agencies in response to their enquiries. In this endeavour, more than 400 
enquiries were responded during the year 2014-15. 
 
WTO/TBT Enquiry Point 
 
BIS continued its activities as the WTO / TBT Enquiry Point.  BIS worked in close interaction 
with the Ministry of Commerce and Industry on various issues of national interest. All valid 
queries pertaining to Standards and Conformity Assessment systems, both national and of 
other countries were replied. 
 
TRAINING SERVICES 
 
During 2014-15, National Institute of Training for Standardization (NITS) organized 53 In-
house programmes, 49 Open programmes including 7 Lead Auditors courses for the 
industry, 4 programs for consumer organizations and 5 programs for Technical Commitee 
Members & Standards Developing Organizations under XII plan thus generating revenue of 
around Rs. 3.62 crores. Besides above, 04 International Training Programmes and 21 
programmes for BIS employees were also organised 
 
CONSUMER AFFAIRS 
 
BIS endeavours to provide its services and the benefits of standardization and certification to 
all its stakeholders in a focused, time bound manner and satisfactory manner. The 
department handles consumer related activities which includes organizing various awareness 
programmes for the stakeholders of BIS, organising Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Award and 
World Standards Day. 
 
Standards Promotion and Consumer Affairs  
 
During the year, 292 consumer awareness programs, 113 Industry awareness programs and 
62 programs for educational utilization of standards were organized. 96 grievances/ 
complaints were received and 80 grievances/complaints were redressed. 
 
Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Award  
 
With a view to encourage manufacturers and service organizations to strive for excellence, 
Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Award was instituted by the Bureau in 1991. The awards for 
Rajiv Gandhi National Quality Awards for the year 2012 were announced by the Hon’ble 
Minister for Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution, Government of India.  The 
awards announced included ‘Best of All Award’ and 03 Category awards (01 Large Scale 
Manufacturing Industry, 01 Large Scale Service Industry and 01 Small Scale Manufacturing

World Standards Day  
 
World Standards Day was celebrated on 14 October 2014 to commemorate the

Enforcement 
 
During the year, 67 enforcement raids were carried out all over India on firms misusing the 
BIS Standard Mark. 18 cases filed in court were decided, all were decided in favour of BIS.

VIGILANCE ACTIVITIES 
 
The vigilance Set up of Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is headed by the Chief Vigilance 
Officer (CVO) and comprises of Vigilance Department at BIS Headquarters. During 2014-15, 
a two-day training programme on Preventive Vigilance for the senior officers of BIS was 
conducted for each of the five regions at NITS, NOIDA, WRO, Mumbai, SRO, Chennai, NRO, 
Chandigarh and ERO, Kolkata during the year. 78 Senior Officers of BIS participated in these 
five training programmes. The Vigilance Awareness Week was celebrated in Bureau of 
Indian Standards from 27 October 2014 to 01 November 2014. 
 
 
HUMAN RESOURCES 
 
During the year, 67 scientists were recruited for the post of Scientist-B by direct recruitment. 
As on 31 March 2015, a total of 1532 persons were on roll in BIS. 
 
INCOME AND EXPENDITURE  
 
For the Twenty Sixth consecutive year i.e. 2014-15, Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) 
continued to be self reliant in meeting its expenditure and other liabilities. Total income 
(excluding income from investment) during the year 2014-15 was Rs. 34542.86 lakh as 
against Rs.31443.12 lakh in the previous year, resulting in an increase of 9.86%. The largest 
contribution to the income was from ISI Certification Marking Fee which stood at Rs. 
30813.14 lakh against Rs. 27949.15 lakh in the previous year i.e. an increase of 10.25%. The 
total revenue expenditure during the year 2014-15 was Rs. 18765.93 lakh as against Rs. 
18656.30 lakh in 2013-14 registering a marginal increase of 0.59%.
