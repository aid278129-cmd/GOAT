# Brag Plan: Zyntrix BIS Compliance Compiler

## Project Identity & Planning Rubric

1. **What is the app?**  
   Zyntrix is an AI-powered deterministic "Product-to-Clause Compliance Compiler" for Indian Standards (BIS/CRS) that converts raw technical product specifications into verified compliance pathways, gap registers, and cryptographic passports with 0% LLM hallucination.

2. **What is the most impressive claim?**  
   "0% LLM authority on compliance verdicts. Truth over guesswork: if there's no evidence, it's missing."

3. **What is the visual hook?**  
   Industrial dark-mode UI (`#070a12`, `#0d121f`), glowing cyan borders (`#00A8E8`), emerald evidence-backed verification tags, and the cryptographic Compliance Passport with SHA-256 seal.

4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?**  
   - Real product assessment: *ThermoSteel Domestic Vacuum Flask 1000ml*
   - Statutory mapping to **IS 17526:2021** & **DPIIT QCO 2023**
   - The Evidence Matrix proving metallurgical composition (IS 6911) and thermal benchmarks (Clause 5.4)
   - The final SHA-256 sealed digital Compliance Passport

5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?**  
   20 seconds (4 scenes, perfectly paced for social share and demo presentation).

6. **What tone fits best?**  
   - **Preset**: `polished`
   - **Creative Direction**: High-stakes regulatory compiler product film — clinical, industrial, uncompromising on truth.

7. **What should the audio feel like?**  
   A confident, modern electronic tech beat (`happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`) with soft UI click accents during card reveals and a final resonant bass hit when the SHA-256 seal lands.

8. **What should the share caption say?**  
   "We built Zyntrix: the deterministic Product-to-Clause Compliance Compiler for Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Zero LLM hallucinations. From product DNA to cryptographic compliance passport in seconds."

9. **What's the user flow worth showing?**  
   - Beat 1: Ingest Product DNA (Extracting physical parameters from PDF)
   - Beat 2: Deterministic Standards Applicability (IS 17526:2021 & Mandatory QCO)
   - Beat 3: Evidence Matrix (NABL Lab reports & Mill Test Certs verified)
   - Beat 4: Cryptographic Passport (Instant audit-ready SHA-256 seal)
