## LinkedIn / Professional Post
Navigating 20,000+ Indian Standards, mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and multi-portal certification schemes is one of the largest regulatory bottlenecks facing Indian hardware manufacturers, MSMEs, and startups.

We built **Zyntrix** — an AI-powered compliance compiler and intelligent assistant for Indian Standards and BIS services:
- **Product DNA Ingestion**: Ingests technical datasheets, CAD specs, and PDFs to extract engineering ground-truth with zero hallucination.
- **Deterministic Standards Applicability**: Automatically maps products to gazetted Indian Standards (e.g., IS 17526:2021) and statutory QCO mandates.
- **Evidence-First Verification Gate**: Proves compliance clause-by-clause against authentic NABL test reports and Mill Test Certificates.
- **Cryptographic Compliance Passport**: Issues an immutable, SHA-256 sealed digital passport ready for factory licensing audit and BIS inspector submission.

Built for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026). Making standards accessible, transparent, and enforceable.
