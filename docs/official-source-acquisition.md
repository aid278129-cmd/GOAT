## 1. Legal & Source Integrity Discipline

The BIS Compliance Compiler is an auditable, evidence-backed regulatory compliance platform. In strict accordance with SIH evaluation rules and ethical engineering:

1. **NO ACCESS CONTROL BYPASS**: We do not bypass paywalls, licensing controls, authentication, captcha, or digital rights management mechanisms on `manakonline.in` or any portal.
2. **NO SCRAPED MIRRORS OR RECONSTRUCTED TEXT**: We do not reconstruct official Indian Standard texts from search snippets, commercial distributor mirrors, or AI generation.
3. **TRANSPARENT PENDING STATUS**: If the complete official standard PDF cannot be legitimately procured within the open development environment, the system explicitly reports:
   $$\text{FULL TEXT ACQUISITION STATE} = \text{OFFICIAL\_DOCUMENT\_ACQUISITION\_PENDING}$$
4. **THREE-WAY METADATA DISTINCTION**:
   - **(A) Official Source Metadata**: Verified portal records from BIS confirming standard number, official title, sectional committee, and amendments.
   - **(B) Official Supporting Material**: Gazette notifications for Quality Control Orders (QCO) and BIS Product Manuals (Scheme of Inspection and Testing).
   - **(C) Official Standard Full Text**: The copyrighted specification document itself.
