# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Compliance Officers, Quality Assurance Engineers, BIS Auditors, and Product Manufacturers. They operate in a high-stakes regulatory environment where precision and traceability are paramount.

## Product Purpose
Zyntrix is a deterministic "Product-to-Clause Compliance Compiler". It evaluates technical product specifications (Product DNA) against statutory BIS/CRS standards to verify compliance, track evidence, identify gaps, and generate certified passports. Success means 100% accurate, evidence-backed deterministic regulatory outcomes with zero LLM hallucination in compliance verdicts.

## Positioning
A deterministic regulatory assurance engine. Unlike standard LLM chatbots that hallucinate compliance scores, Zyntrix strictly routes product facts through a verifiable, 9-layer compliance architecture, proving every claim with verifiable artifacts.

## Operating Context
Users evaluate hardware specifications against BIS gazette requirements. The workflow involves onboarding product DNA, scoping standard applicability, mapping requirements to product facts, uploading artifact evidence (lab tests), identifying gaps for certified lab dispatch, and issuing a final cryptographic passport.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 9-layer deterministic backend architecture.
- 0% LLM authority on compliance decisions.
- Must maintain a clean, industrial, technical, trustworthy, and evidence-oriented UX.
- Fully runnable frontend (Vite/React) and backend (FastAPI/Python).

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Zyntrix BIS Compliance Compiler
- Voice: Professional, industrial, clinical, and uncompromising on truth.
- Visuals: Clean, dense but readable layouts, technical aesthetics, without generic visual slop.

## Evidence on Hand
- Regulatory frameworks: Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) / CRS Scheme.
- Data structures for: Product DNA, Applicability mapping, Clause evaluation, Lab routing.

## Product Principles
1. **Truth Over Guesswork:** If there's no evidence, it's missing. The system never guesses.
2. **Traceability:** Every decision must be traceable back to the official standard.
3. **Clarity:** The interface must surface the exact gap blocking compliance without overwhelming the user with raw backend logic unless requested.
